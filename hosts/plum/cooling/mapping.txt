Names: group letter first, then position (cin/cou CPU inner/outer,
r*/t*/f*/b* rear/top/front/bottom).

CPU fans:
  cin: inner 120mm, mb2, fan2 on tach, up to 2000, 0 keeps it spinning at ~250
  cou: outer 120mm, mb5, fan5 on tach, up to 2000, 0 keeps it spinning at ~250

Rear case fans:
  rto: top 120mm, fc7, fan7 on tach, up to ~3000
  r4t: top 40mm, mb3, fan3 on tach, up to ~3300
  r4m: middle 40mm, mb1, fan1 on tach, up to ~3550
  r4b: bottom 40mm, mb4, fan4 on tach, up to ~3100

Top case fans:
  tre: rear 140mm, fc6, fan6 on tach, up to ~2410
  tfr: front 140mm, fc1, fan1 on tach, up to ~2410

Front case fans:
  fto: top 120mm, fc2, fan2 on tach, up to ~3050
  fmi: mid 140mm, fc3, fan3 on tach, up to ~2440
  fbo: bot 140mm, fc4, fan4 on tach, up to ~2380

Bottom case fans:
  bma: main 120mm, fc5, fan5 on tach, up to ~3000 (ringing above ~2150?)
  ble: left ~60mm, fc10, fan10 on tach, up to ~2765
  bri: right ~60mm, fc9, fan9 on tach, up to ~2735
